Cold Email Writer settings

Everything here stays in your browser. Changes apply to the next email you write.

Your template

Put anything that changes per person in brackets, like [FIRST NAME], [FIRM], and [PERSONALIZED LINE]. Everything outside brackets is kept exactly as written.

Keep in touch template

Used when you pick Keep in touch in the side panel, for people you've already talked to. Same bracket rules as above.

About you

Used to find things you share with each person and to write hooks that sound like you.

Target schools

If someone went to one of these schools (and not Rutgers), cold emails leave Rutgers out and say "a second-year student studying finance and math" instead. Separate schools with commas.

Style notes

Tone, length, and anything the emails should always or never do.

Deal search

Finds deals their group worked on so the email can open with one, like the EnergyCAP example. Each email takes about 30 seconds instead of a few, and costs a bit more. When this is off, Dig deeper does the same search on demand.

Example emails

Paste 2 or 3 cold emails you've sent and liked, separated by a line with ---. This is the most reliable way to make every email sound the same.

Gmail

Email formats

The To field fills in automatically for about 90 banks, like flast@jefferies.com. Add firms that are missing, or fix ones that are wrong, one per line. Your lines take priority. Use first, last, f (first initial), m (middle initial), and l (last initial).

Apollo (optional)

Adds a Find with Apollo button next to the To field. Each lookup uses 1 Apollo credit and only runs when you click it. In Apollo, go to Settings, Integrations, API Keys, and create a master key.

Claude API

Create one at console.anthropic.com. Dig deeper also needs web search turned on in the Console.
